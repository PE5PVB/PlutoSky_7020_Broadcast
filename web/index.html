DAB+

Multiplex–

In DAB+ the TX bandwidth is 3 MHz by itself.

The DAB+ multiplex: up to 8 services in 864 capacity units (CU). One service can be encoded here (the I2S input or a test tone); the others arrive already encoded from ODR-AudioEnc over ZeroMQ (odr-audioenc … -o tcp://<pluto>:<port>). Apply stores the layout; on the air labels change at once, everything else through an announced reconfiguration about 3 s later, so radios follow without a new scan.

Services

Protection: EEP-3A is the usual level (rate 1/2); 1A/1B protect most and cost most capacity, 4A/4B least. EEP-B needs a bitrate that is a multiple of 32. A service that keeps its SId, bitrate and protection plays on without a gap through a reconfiguration. The bitrate of a ZeroMQ service is the sender's (odr-audioenc -b): when the sender changes it, the multiplex follows by itself. Slideshow: tells radios that the PAD carries MOT slides (FIG 0/13). Anyone who can reach a ZeroMQ port can feed that service: open it to the internet only towards the encoder's address.

Slideshow (service encoded here)

–
Next slide every s

Up to 8 images, shown in turn on radios with a screen; a new or replaced one goes on the air first. The browser scales each image to 320 × 240 (the slideshow size; black bars where the shape differs) and stores it as JPEG of at most about 30 kB. The slides travel in the PAD of the service encoded here and take part of its bitrate while they are sent; the time per slide follows from its size and that bitrate.

TX calibration (expert)

Last LO nulling–
Carrier S/N (residual FM of the silent carrier at the last nulling; 75 kHz ref, 50 µs, 30 Hz–15 kHz, unweighted)–

Digital level, DC and I/Q correction apply to the DAB+ signal as well. LO nulling runs in FM (it needs the silent carrier); the values it finds stay in use here.

Digital correction of the analog LO leakage and I/Q image, stored in the Pluto. Nulling procedure: set an offset of 100 kHz, then adjust DC I/Q for the lowest line at the LO frequency (carrier minus the offset) and gain/skew for the lowest image (carrier minus 2 x the offset) on a spectrum analyser; then set the offset back to 0 or keep it. Gain/skew need bitstream B1D00019 or later.

Mask monitor

Power meter (RX1)

Calibration offset–

Measuring bridge only (nothing is controlled by it): connect the transmitter output through an external attenuator to RX1 and enter its attenuation above. The AD9361 RX input is damaged above about +2.5 dBm: keep the level at RX1 below −10 dBm (the meter shows a warning above that). The nominal calibration is an estimate (about ±2 dB); calibrate once against a known power for an accurate reading.

Audio tone meter

Strongest tone–
Deviation–

Demodulates the own transmission (TX1 through the directional coupler → RX2). Frequency and deviation of the strongest audio tone, one value per 0.25 s. Use it with the encoder's test generator for level sweeps.

End-to-end impulse response

Statusidle

Start the encoder's impulse mode first (one frame of 20–40 % full scale per second, otherwise silent or pilot only). The mask monitor pauses meanwhile. The result is the response from I2S frame to the demodulated RX2 signal: magnitude relative to 1 kHz, phase and group delay.
